Chat and voice are scored on 9 quality metrics, including hallucination, bias, completeness, context awareness, response quality and conversation flow. Each carries High, Medium or Low confidence and an evidence excerpt. Sessions end when the goal is met, the turn limit is reached or the agent fails unrecoverably.
