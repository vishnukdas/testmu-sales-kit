1
Auto-Heal

Recovers a broken element on every run. The test itself does not change.

2
Adaptive Heal

Re-authors from the failing step and saves a version for you to approve.

3
Retry on failure

Reruns the test, up to five times, before calling it failed.

Auto-approve is off by default, because re-authoring a step could turn a real defect into a pass. Adaptive Heal and Dynamic Test apply to New Experience tests on Chrome. Healing uses credits when it recovers a step.
